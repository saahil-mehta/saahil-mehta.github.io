# Surface brief: /showreel

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters and hiring managers who want to see the work itself in under two minutes, then peers who read on.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work plays as a reel inside the title-sequence world: the product leads the first screen, then one scene per screen tells Orca and the platform under it. It refuses the case-study template of hero image, problem, solution and stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: The site's field and inks, Mona Sans only, vermilion spent on the chapter in view and in-build marks. The Orca reconstruction is a lifted panel in the same inks with the product's own structure (bar, conversation, steps, table, artefact, composer); the Orca mark is a one-ink silhouette.

STORY: The visitor sees Orca answer a real kind of question, learns how it works and what it does, reads adoption and running cost as sentences, watches the platform improve from 2023 to 2026, then sees the savings, the drawing and the plan. They leave believing one person designed and shipped both, and book a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: the Orca mark, "Orca" at display size, the one-line claim, the date the figures stand at. Right seven columns: the reconstruction playing its exchange. The chapter bar sits at the foot of the window; Book a call stays in the header.

FORM: Full-screen title-sequence scenes with a chapter scrubber; first of the three dealt structures, seed 15a8fd53.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
